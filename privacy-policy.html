1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app Easy QR: Scanner & Generator ("Easy QR", "the app") handles information. Easy QR scans QR codes and barcodes, creates QR codes and barcodes, and keeps an optional history on your device.

Scanning and code creation are processed on your device and work without an internet connection. Easy QR has no user accounts and no servers of its own, and the developer does not receive the codes you scan or create or your history. The app does, however, contain Google software development kits (SDKs) for advertising and barcode recognition, which collect some information as described in sections 9 and 10. Easy QR is therefore not a "no data collected" app.

4. Camera permission and QR/barcode scanning

  • The camera is used only to detect QR codes and barcodes. Scanning starts automatically on the Scan screen; there is no photo capture.
  • Camera frames are analysed in memory by Google ML Kit's barcode model, which is bundled inside the app and runs on your device. Frames are not saved and are not sent anywhere by Easy QR.
  • The camera is released when you leave the Scan screen. The flashlight and zoom controls only change camera settings.
  • You can refuse or revoke camera access at any time in Android Settings. You can still scan from a photo and create codes without it.

5. Gallery image access

"Scan from photo" uses the Android system photo picker. Easy QR only receives the single image you select; it does not request permission to read your photo library. The image is decoded and scanned in memory on your device, then discarded. It is not copied, stored or uploaded.

6. Local scan and generation history

When Save history is on (the default), Easy QR stores each scanned or created code in a database inside the app's private storage on your device. An entry contains the code content, its format and type (for example "Website" or "EAN-13"), a short title, the date and time, and whether it was scanned or generated.

History is never uploaded by Easy QR. You can search it, delete individual entries, clear all history, or turn off Save history in Settings so new codes are not recorded.

7. Wi-Fi QR generation and password handling

  • Wi-Fi QR codes are created only from the network name, password and security type that you type in. Easy QR does not read the Wi-Fi networks or passwords saved on your device.
  • By default, Wi-Fi passwords are removed before an entry is saved to history. They are kept only if you turn on Save Wi-Fi passwords in Settings.
  • When you scan a Wi-Fi code, "Connect to network" opens Android's own add-network dialog (Android 11 and later), where you decide whether to save the network.
  • On Android 13 and later, copied Wi-Fi passwords are marked as sensitive so the system's clipboard preview hides them.

8. Generated QR codes and exported files

  • Codes are generated on your device.
  • Save PNG opens the Android document picker. The image is written only to the location you choose and is then under your control.
  • Share creates a temporary image in the app's cache (replaced the next time you share) and hands it to the app you pick in the Android share sheet. Once shared, the receiving app's own privacy practices apply.
  • Copy places the content on the Android clipboard, where other apps you use may be able to read it.

9. Advertising and Google AdMob

Easy QR is free and shows ads (banners and occasional full-screen ads) provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To request, show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect information from your device, including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • app interaction information, such as app launches, taps and ad views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app performance and ad-loading data;
  • device and advertising identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID, and, on supported devices, Android Privacy Sandbox signals (for example Topics and attribution), depending on your device settings and consent choices.

Depending on your choices and location, ads may be personalised or non-personalised. Easy QR does not send the content of your scans, created codes or history to Google or any advertiser. Google's use of this information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

10. Information collected by third-party SDKs

Google Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform (Google)

As described in section 9, plus your consent choices where a consent form is required. See Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Google ML Kit barcode scanning

The barcode model runs on your device. According to Google, ML Kit may send diagnostic and usage information, such as device model and OS version, app package name and version, performance metrics, API configuration, error codes and a per-installation identifier that is not intended to identify you, to Google over HTTPS. Google states that it does not share this data with third parties. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

11. Consent management and advertising preferences

  • Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), Easy QR uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before ads are requested. Ads are only requested once your choice permits it.
  • When it applies to you, Settings → Ad privacy options lets you review or change your choice at any time.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID and limit ad personalisation in Android Settings (Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads, depending on your device).
  • You can manage Google ad personalisation at My Ad Center.

12. Data storage and security

  • History and settings are stored in Easy QR's private app storage, which Android isolates from other apps. Easy QR does not add its own encryption to this data. Anyone with access to your unlocked device can open the app and see your history.
  • Easy QR allows Android's standard app backup. Depending on your device and backup settings, your history and settings may be included in your device or Google account backup and restored to a new device.
  • Easy QR itself does not transmit your history or code content. Data collected by Google SDKs is sent to Google, which states that it is encrypted in transit.
  • No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Data retention and deletion

  • History entries remain on your device until you delete them, clear history, clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app.
  • To delete: open History and long-press an entry → Delete; or use the bin icon on the History screen or Settings → Clear all history.
  • Files you saved or shared are outside Easy QR and must be deleted where you saved or sent them.
  • The developer holds no copy of your data and therefore cannot retrieve or delete it on your behalf. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

14. Children's privacy

Easy QR is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in their country). Ads in the app are not configured for child-directed treatment. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, please contact us.

Owner review: confirm this matches the target audience and age ratings declared in the Google Play Console.

15. External links and third-party services

Scanned codes may contain web addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, map locations or contact details. Easy QR never opens them automatically. It shows the content first, and only opens a browser, email, dialer, maps or contacts app when you choose an action. Those websites and apps are operated by third parties and have their own privacy policies, and Easy QR has no control over them. Review links before opening them.
